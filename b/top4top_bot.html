Download Bots on Telegram: A Comparative Look at Top4toP and Its Rivals

With hundreds of bots available, choosing the right one depends on your specific needs—whether it’s unlimited storage, speed, or cross-platform support.

How Top4toP Compares to Other Download Bots

This makes it more of a file hosting bot than a downloader in the traditional sense.
