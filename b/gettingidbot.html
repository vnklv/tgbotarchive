Overview of ID Bots

ID bots typically work by: - Forwarding a message from a user, group, or channel to the bot. - Using inline queries or commands to retrieve IDs. - Providing additional metadata like account creation dates, usernames, or bio information.

The best ID bots are fast, accurate, and handle private chats, groups, and channels without errors. They are especially valuable for developers debugging bots or admins managing large communities.
