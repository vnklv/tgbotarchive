Overview of @Ya_FooterBot

Ya_FooterBot (@Ya_FooterBot) is a specialized bot that automatically adds customizable footers to channel posts. Footers can include links, signatures, or useful info like contact details or calls-to-action. Key features include signature rotation (cycling through multiple footers), ad filtering (to avoid mixing promotional content with organic posts), flexible settings (per-channel or per-post customization), and tag control (managing hashtags or mentions). It's built for efficiency, helping channels save time, boost sales, and attract subscribers through consistent branding.
