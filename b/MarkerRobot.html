Overview of the Telegram Bot Ecosystem

Telegram bots act as mini-applications, running inside the chat interface. They can moderate, automate, or provide services like VPN access, monitoring, or digital purchases. The bots in this comparison serve very different purposes, making direct feature comparisons challenging but highlighting their niche strengths.

How @MarkerRobot Compares to Other Bots

@MarkerRobot focuses exclusively on watermarking channel posts – adding a signature or logo to every image, video, or document. It offers granular control over watermark position, opacity, size, and rotation, and supports multiple formats. This is a niche but essential tool for content creators who want to protect their work from unauthorized copying.

In contrast, the other bots address entirely different needs: - @Mostvpnio_bot provides a Telegram-native VPN for accessing blocked content in Russia, using VLESS+Reality protocol. - @SpyTrackedBot and @SpyFreeRobot monitor deleted messages and self-destructing media, useful for security or curiosity. - @BERXSTRAX_SHOP_BOT and @TGivo_bot enable purchasing in-game donations or Telegram Stars, premium features, and giveaways.

None of these bots overlap with watermarking, so @MarkerRobot is the only choice for channel branding.

Key Strengths and Unique Features of @MarkerRobot

  • Extensive customization: Adjust watermark position (9 preset spots or custom coordinates), opacity (0-100%), scale, rotation, and repetition across images.
  • Batch processing: Automatically watermarks all new posts in a channel, not just individual uploads.
  • Format support: Works with images (JPEG, PNG, GIF), videos (MP4), and documents (PDF).
  • User-friendly setup: Simple commands like /setwatermark with a photo or text, and /settings for advanced tweaks.
  • No privacy risks: The bot only sees posts in channels it’s added to, not private chats.

Its main limitation is being single-purpose – it cannot do anything beyond watermarking.
