Blacklist Bots on Telegram: A Comparative Look at AntiDangerBot and Its Rivals

Telegram groups, especially larger communities, often face threats from spammers, scammers, and malicious users. Blacklist bots have emerged as a specialized tool to combat this. These bots allow group admins to share, sync, and enforce bans based on a community-driven database of reported "dangerous" users. By leveraging collective reporting, they aim to prevent threats before they enter a group. While many general moderation bots exist, dedicated blacklist bots focus purely on this sharing and enforcement mechanism.
