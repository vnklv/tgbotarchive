ThirtyOne Bot: A Classic Card Game Reimagined

@ThirtyOneBot brings the traditional card game “31” to Telegram. The premise is simple: players aim to be the last one standing over multiple rounds. Each round starts with three cards dealt to each player. On your turn, you can swap a card from your hand with a card from the deck, trying to build the best possible hand. The goal is to avoid having the lowest hand value when a round ends, as that player loses a “life.” The last player with lives remaining wins.

This bot excels at replicating the tension and strategy of the physical card game, with clear turn-based play and a straightforward interface. It’s perfect for small groups who enjoy classic, social card games.
