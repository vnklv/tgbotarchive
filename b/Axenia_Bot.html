Overview of Axenia (@Axenia_Bot)

Axenia is a straightforward karma bot that allows group members to give positive or negative reputation points to each other. It tracks these scores and displays them in a leaderboard, fostering a sense of community and encouraging constructive participation. Unlike complex polling bots, Axenia focuses solely on peer-to-peer reputation.
