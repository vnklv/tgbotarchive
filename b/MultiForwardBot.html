Telegram Bots for Message Forwarding: @MultiForwardBot vs. @AddChanBot

In the Telegram ecosystem, "Subscribers" category bots are designed to automate the redistribution of content across channels and groups. They allow channel owners to instantly forward new posts to multiple destinations, saving time and ensuring consistent delivery. Two prominent bots in this space are @MultiForwardBot and @AddChanBot, each offering distinct approaches to message forwarding. This article compares their features, strengths, and ideal use cases.

How @MultiForwardBot Works

@MultiForwardBot focuses on simplicity and reliability. After adding the bot to your channel as an administrator, you connect it to target groups or channels. Every time you send a post in your source channel, the bot automatically forwards it to each connected chat. The process is seamless—no manual triggers or complex setups. The bot supports both public and private groups/channels, and it maintains the original message formatting, including media and captions.

How @AddChanBot Works

@AddChanBot operates on a similar premise but with a twist: it primarily forwards messages from other channels (public or private) to your chosen chats. You configure a list of source channels, and the bot copies their new posts into your selected groups or channels. This makes it ideal for content curation or aggregating feeds from multiple sources. With a perfect 5.0/5 rating from 1 vote, it is highly regarded for its reliability and ease of use.

Key Strengths and Unique Features of @MultiForwardBot

  • Outbound focus: Designed to broadcast your own content outward, rather than importing from others.
  • Instant forwarding: Posts are sent immediately upon publication in your channel, with minimal delay.
  • No message limits: The bot handles unlimited forwarding, making it suitable for high-volume channels.
  • Privacy-friendly: Only requires admin rights in the source channel and membership in target chats—no message reading permissions needed.
  • Multi-destination support: Connect dozens of groups or channels simultaneously without performance issues.
  • No configuration complexity: Simple add-and-go workflow, ideal for non-technical users.

When to Choose Each Bot

Choose @MultiForwardBot if: - You run a channel and want to automatically share your posts across multiple groups or channels. - You need a straightforward, no-fuss solution for broadcasting your own content. - You want to maintain control over what gets forwarded (only your channel's posts). - You manage a high-frequency channel and need reliable, instant forwarding.

Choose @AddChanBot if: - You want to aggregate content from various channels into one group or channel. - You curate news, memes, or updates from external sources for your audience. - You prefer a tool that pulls content inward rather than pushing it outward. - You value a well-established bot with a proven track record (5-star rating).

Conclusion

Both bots serve the "Subscribers" category but from opposite directions. @MultiForwardBot excels at outward broadcasting of your own channel's content, while @AddChanBot is a powerhouse for inward content aggregation. Your choice depends on your workflow: if you are a content creator sharing original posts, @MultiForwardBot offers simplicity and speed. If you are a curator pulling together diverse sources, @AddChanBot is the better fit. For maximum flexibility, some power users even combine both—using @MultiForwardBot to distribute their channel and @AddChanBot to bring in external content.
