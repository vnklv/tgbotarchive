The Noforward Bot Showdown: ForwardiBot vs. the Competition

Telegram’s “forwarded from” tag can clutter channels and break the illusion of original content. Noforward bots solve this by stripping that tag, reposting messages as clean originals. They’re essential for news aggregators, brand channels, and any admin who wants a seamless feed. Among the options, ForwardiBot (@ForwardiBot) stands out, but how does it compare to established rivals like @clean_forward_bot and niche tools like @HidEgoBot?

What Noforward Bots Do

These bots typically work by: - Receiving forwarded messages from a source chat. - Removing the “Forwarded from” header and sender attribution. - Reposting the cleaned message to a target channel or group. - Some offer additional filters (e.g., media-only, text-only) or privacy modes.

The core value is content hygiene: viewers see the message as if it were posted directly, without the visual baggage of forwarding.

ForwardiBot: Core Strengths

ForwardiBot focuses on simplicity and reliability. Its key features include: - Zero configuration: Add the bot to your channel as an admin, then forward messages to it. It cleans and reposts automatically. - Media preservation: Images, videos, documents, and polls retain their original formatting. - No user tracking: Unlike some bots, ForwardiBot doesn’t log who forwarded what—just processes the message. - Lightweight: No commands or settings to tweak. It’s a set-and-forget tool.

The Competitors

@clean_forward_bot (rated 5.0/5 with 100k+ votes) is the market leader. It offers: - Works in both private chats and channels. - Supports multiple languages. - Allows you to set a custom “via” signature or remove it entirely. - Has a paid tier for advanced features like scheduled posts.

@HidEgoBot (rated 1.0/5, 1 vote) is an open-source, privacy-focused alternative. It lets you create anonymous messaging bots but is not a dedicated noforward tool. Its low rating suggests bugs or limited functionality.

When to Choose Each Bot

  • Choose ForwardiBot if: You want a dead-simple, no-frills solution for a single channel. It’s perfect for beginners or admins who just need clean reposts without extra features. Its lack of configuration is a strength for minimalists.
  • Choose clean_forward_bot if: You manage multiple channels, need private chat support, or want to add a custom “via” signature. Its high rating and extensive user base make it a safe bet for power users.
  • Avoid HidEgoBot for noforward tasks: It’s not designed for this purpose. Its low rating and complexity make it unsuitable unless you specifically need an anonymous relay bot.

Final Verdict

ForwardiBot fills a niche for those who value simplicity over features. For most channel admins, clean_forward_bot remains the gold standard—but if you’re tired of tweaking settings, give ForwardiBot a try. It does one thing and does it well.
