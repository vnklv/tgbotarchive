Force Subscription Bots for Telegram Groups: A Comparative Look

### What Force Bots Do

All force bots operate on a simple principle: when a user tries to send a message in a group, the bot checks if they are a member of a designated channel. If not, the bot mutes or restricts them and sends a prompt to join. Once joined, the user can post freely. This mechanism is widely used to grow channels organically while keeping groups clean.
