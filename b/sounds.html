What Music Telegram Bots Typically Do

The majority of music bots on Telegram are downloaders. Speed, format options (MP3, FLAC), and platform support are their main selling points. In contrast, a sound library bot like @sounds focuses on providing pre-curated audio clips for messaging, not full-length tracks.
