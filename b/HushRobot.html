### What Mute Bots Do

Mute bots generally allow admins to prevent all members from sending messages in a group for a set duration. This is useful for announcements, events, or managing spam bursts. Unlike full moderation suites, they skip complex permissions and focus on a single toggle. However, some bots in this category also offer additional features like captcha verification, video conversion, or channel subscription enforcement—straying from pure muting.
