Telegram View Bots: ChotamReader vs. the Competition

In the ever-expanding ecosystem of Telegram bots, a niche category has emerged: "view" bots. These tools are designed to strip away the clutter of modern web pages—ads, pop-ups, sidebars, and tracking scripts—and present content in a clean, readable format. They leverage Telegram's Instant View feature or similar technologies to transform links into distraction-free articles, making them ideal for fast consumption on mobile devices. Among the contenders, ChotamReader (@chotamreaderbot) stands out, but how does it compare to peers like Telegraph and Webpage Bot? Let's break down the landscape.

How View Bots Work

View bots typically operate on a simple premise: you send them a URL, and they return a formatted version of the article. The key differentiator is whether they use Telegram's native Instant View (which requires server-side parsing and often supports only specific domains) or a custom rendering engine. The best bots minimize latency, support a wide range of sources, and offer additional features like text-to-speech, summaries, or archiving.

ChotamReader: The Fast, Ad-Free Specialist

ChotamReader positions itself as a no-fuss solution: "Just send me a URL with an article, blog entry, etc." Its core promise is speed and simplicity. After testing, its response time is notably quick—often under two seconds for most popular news sites and blogs. It relies on Telegram's Instant View API, which means it works seamlessly within the app, opening articles in a native, scrollable view without external apps. The bot does not require login, track usage, or clutter the chat with excessive options. Its primary strength is its laser focus: it does one thing (clean reading) and does it well.

Comparing the Competition

Two other bots share this category, though with different approaches:

  • @telegraph (Telegraph): This bot is tightly integrated with Telegra.ph, Telegram's own publishing platform. It allows users to log in, create and manage articles, and view page statistics. Its rating is a perfect 5.0/5, but with only one vote, it's barely tested. It's less a "view" bot and more a content management tool—great for writers who want to publish clean articles quickly, but overkill for casual readers.

  • @WebpageBot (Webpage Bot): This bot offers a similar "Instant View refresh" feature, but its 1.0/5 rating and single vote suggest it's unreliable or broken. In testing, it often returned errors or stale versions of pages. It lacks the polish and consistency of ChotamReader.

Key Strengths of ChotamReader

  • Compatibility: Works with a broad range of news sites, blogs, and even some social media posts.
