Telegram Administration Bots: Keeping Your Groups in Check

Telegram groups grow fast, and managing them can become a full-time job. Administration bots automate the heavy lifting—from muting unruly members to verifying new users, banning spammers, and even adding captions to channel posts. These bots are essential for admins who want to maintain order without being online 24/7. In this article, we compare Group Butler (@cv_groupbutlerbot) with five other popular administration bots, highlighting what each does best.

The Contenders

  • MuteAllGroupsBot (@MuteAllGroupsBot) – Focuses on one thing: muting and unmuting all groups you connect. Simple, but limited.

When to Choose Each Bot

  • Choose Group Butler if it comes back online and you want a single bot for basic moderation tasks. Ideal for small to medium groups.
  • Choose MuteAllGroupsBot if your only need is to quickly mute or unmute all your groups at once. It's simple and reliable.
  • Choose BanVoteBot for groups that want democratic moderation. Members can ban spammers without admin intervention.
  • Choose MissZarin_bot for managing multiple groups with shared ban lists. Perfect for communities with many related groups.
  • Choose ButterBot when security is your top priority. Captchas and auto-kick keep bots out effectively.
  • Choose Channel Automation 2 if you run a Telegram channel and need automatic captions or reactions on posts.

Final Thoughts

While Group Butler offers a promising all-in-one solution, its offline status makes it unreliable for active groups.
