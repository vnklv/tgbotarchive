Todo Bots on Telegram: A Comparative Guide

Telegram bots in the "todo" category help users manage tasks, reminders, and lists directly within the chat interface. They range from simple reminder tools to advanced list managers that integrate with hashtags and inline mode. The key is finding a bot that matches your workflow—whether you need cross-device sync, group collaboration, or automated list creation from messages.

Overview of @todogroup_bot

todobot (@todogroup_bot) stands out for its hashtag-driven list management. It automatically creates separate lists based on hashtags like #bugs, #movies, or any custom tag you define. You can manage these lists in private chats or groups, and use inline mode to quickly add items. This makes it ideal for users who want to organize messages without manually sorting them.

How @todogroup_bot Compares to Competitors

While most todo bots focus on simple task entry or reminders, todobot excels at automated categorization. For example, if you forward a message with #shopping to the bot, it instantly adds it to your shopping list. Other bots require you to manually type tasks or set reminders, which can be slower for high-volume organization.

However, todobot lacks built-in reminder features (like daily or recurring alerts) that competitors such as @daily_remind_me_bot or @yastabot offer. It also doesn’t support cross-device synchronization out-of-box like @sp_adv_reminder_bot does, though Telegram’s cloud sync applies to all bots.

Key Strengths and Unique Features of @todogroup_bot

  • Hashtag-based list creation: Automatically sorts tasks into lists based on hashtags—no manual sorting needed.
  • Group and private chat support: Manage lists in both contexts, useful for teams or personal use.
  • Inline mode: Quickly add items without opening the bot chat.
  • Custom lists: Create any list you want beyond predefined categories.
  • Zero learning curve: Works naturally with how you already use Telegram (e.g., tagging messages).

When to Choose Each Bot

  • Choose @todogroup_bot if: You frequently organize messages by topic (e.g., work, personal, projects) and want automatic list creation. Ideal for power users who hashtag everything.
  • Choose @sp_adv_reminder_bot if: You need reliable cross-device sync and simple reminders without hashtags.
  • Choose @ntosbot if: Your primary need is to save and forward messages (including media) to other chats via inline.
  • Choose @daily_remind_me_bot or @yastabot if: You need recurring or one-time reminders with notifications (e.g., daily standups, birthdays).
  • Choose @do2bot if: You want to create and share todo lists with others, and prefer a widely-used bot (27M+ votes).

Final Thoughts

@todogroup_bot fills a unique niche for hashtag-driven organization, making it a standout choice for users who already use tags in Telegram. It complements rather than replaces other bots—use todobot for list creation, and pair it with @daily_remind_me_bot for reminders if needed. For most users, the best approach is to try todobot for automatic sorting and add a dedicated reminder bot for time-based tasks.
