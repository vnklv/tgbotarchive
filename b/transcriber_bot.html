### What Voice Bots Do

  • Voice bots typically handle one or more of these tasks:
  • **Speech-to-text**: Transcribe voice messages into readable text.
  • **Text-to-speech**: Convert written text into spoken audio.
  • **Sound sharing**: Provide libraries of sound effects or voice clips.
  • **File conversion**: Turn text into audio files (e.g., MP3).

TranscriberBot uniquely combines speech-to-text with image transcription, a rare feature in this category.
