What Search Bots Do

Search Telegram bots typically fall into two categories: content retrieval and data discovery. Content retrieval bots let you save messages, stickers, or media with keywords and pull them up instantly via inline search. Data discovery bots, on the other hand, help you find public information about users or groups. All bots in this comparison offer inline search capabilities, but their core functions vary widely.
