Bots for Book Lovers on Telegram: A Practical Guide

What Books Bots Do (In a Nutshell)

Most books bots on Telegram fall into three camps: - Discovery & info bots – They give you book details, ratings, and reviews (like Good Reads Books Bot). - Download bots – They let you find and retrieve free ebooks from sources like Z-Library or Library Genesis. - Utility bots – They modify ebook metadata, such as covers or author tags.

The download bots are popular but often face availability issues, while utility bots are niche. Good Reads Books Bot takes a different approach: it’s an inline bot focused on information, not file sharing.

Compared to the download-heavy bots in the same category, it’s a different beast.
