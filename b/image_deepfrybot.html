Overview of @image_deepfrybot

Image Deepfry Bot is a specialized tool that applies the infamous "deepfry" effect to images. This effect, popular in internet meme culture, artificially over-saturates colors, reduces quality, adds grain, and often includes exaggerated contrast—making the image look burnt, distorted, and intentionally ugly. The bot accepts direct image uploads and URLs, making it versatile. Its simplicity is its charm: you send a picture, and it returns a hilariously ruined version.
