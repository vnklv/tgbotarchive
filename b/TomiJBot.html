Overview of Tomi (@TomiJBot)

Tomi is a group-oriented puzzle bot that immerses players in a story where they must solve riddles and enigmas to help a character named Tomi escape. It thrives on collaboration—players work together to decipher clues, making it ideal for friend groups or communities seeking a shared mental challenge. The bot emphasizes logic, wordplay, and lateral thinking, with a clear goal-driven narrative.

How Tomi Compares to Other Game Bots
