What Do Telegraph Bots Do?

At their core, telegraph bots act as bridges between Telegram and Telegra.ph, a minimalist publishing tool by Telegram. Users send content (text, media, or links) to the bot, which then creates a formatted Telegra.ph page. The resulting link can be shared with anyone, even non-Telegram users. These bots vary in specialization: some handle code, others focus on media, and a few offer analytics or lyric extraction.

CodeShare Bot: Features and Current Status

This simplicity makes it ideal for developers or writers who need quick, uncluttered sharing. However, the bot is currently flagged as offline, meaning it may not respond to commands. Key strengths of CodeShare Bot include: - Code-friendly formatting: Preserves indentation and syntax, unlike generic text bots. - No frills: No need to log in; just send and save. - Privacy: No account linking—perfect for one-off shares.

But the offline flag significantly undermines its utility, making alternatives more attractive.
