What Twitter Telegram Bots Do

These bots generally fall into two camps: - Preview bots: Automatically expand X links in group chats, showing the tweet content (text, images, video) as a rich preview, so users don't have to click away. - Downloader bots: Let users send an X link and receive the media (video, image, GIF) directly in chat, often with options for quality.

Some bots combine both functions. The key differentiators are ease of use, feature set, privacy, and reliability.
