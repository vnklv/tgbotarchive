What Do Checker Bots Actually Do?

At their core, checker bots automate the process of verifying information. They eliminate the need for manual checks, providing instant feedback within the Telegram interface. The category is broad, covering:

  • Security checks: Scanning files and links for malware (e.g., @DrWebBot).
  • Account status checks: Looking up PSN IDs or checking for data breaches (e.g., @psnchecker_bot, @pwned_robot).
  • Restriction checks: Identifying limitations on bots, groups, or channels (e.g., @checkrestrictionsbot).
  • Availability checks: Monitoring if a website or service is online (e.g., @MonitorBot_bot).

Each bot specializes in a specific niche, but MonitorBot's focus on uptime monitoring is both its greatest strength and its narrowest limitation.
