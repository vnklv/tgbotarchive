What Are Customizable Telegram Bots?

Customizable Telegram bots are powerful tools that allow users to create their own automated content-filtering or management bots without writing code. These bots typically offer features like database integration, forced channel subscriptions, auto-deletion, and advertising systems. They are popular among Telegram group and channel administrators who want to maintain content quality, automate routine tasks, or monetize their communities. The "customizable" category stands out because it gives users granular control over features, often through a web dashboard or inline commands, rather than relying on pre-built, rigid templates.
