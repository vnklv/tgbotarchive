React Bot (@ReactBot) vs. Top Comments Bots: A Practical Comparison

Telegram bots in the "comments" category serve a simple but powerful purpose: they allow channel admins to add interactive feedback buttons (like 👍, 👎, ❤️) or full comment sections directly to their posts. This turns one-way broadcasts into engaging conversations, boosting user retention and community feel. With many options available, choosing the right one can be tricky. Here’s how React Bot (@ReactBot) stacks up against its main competitors.

Overview of the Category

Comments bots generally work by creating a linked "comment group" for each channel post. Users react or write messages in that group, and the bot forwards them back to the channel as buttons or inline replies. Some bots focus on simple reactions, while others offer full management suites. The goal is always to increase interaction without forcing users to leave Telegram.

How @ReactBot Compares

React Bot focuses on adding feedback buttons (like 👍, 👎, ❤️, 😂) to channel posts. It’s designed for simplicity—you send a message to the bot, it gives you a link or button set to paste into your channel. No complex setup, no group management. This makes it ideal for quick polls, sentiment checks, or simple engagement.

Other bots in the category offer more but often at the cost of complexity: - @CommentsBot (126 billion votes) is the giant—it creates full comment sections with threaded replies, but requires linking a group and managing permissions. - @ComenBot (11.6 million votes) is similar but lighter, focusing on creating "comment messages" that can be forwarded anywhere. - @ChannelButtonsBot (flagged offline) offered reaction buttons but is currently unavailable. - @Manageil_bot is a full management bot—it can add views, likes, and comments, but also kicks, bans, and broadcasts. - @NoGCommentsBot is a niche tool to delete comments from users who haven’t joined your group.

Key Strengths of React Bot

  • Ease of use: No group linking, no admin panels. Send a message, get a button.
  • Lightweight: Doesn’t clutter your channel with extra management features.
  • Privacy-friendly: Users don’t need to join a group to leave a reaction.
  • Speed: Reactions appear instantly as buttons on your post.
  • Perfect for small channels: If you just want a "like" button, this is the simplest solution.

Recommendations on When to Choose Each Bot

  • Choose @ReactBot if: You want a dead-simple way to add like/dislike or emoji reactions to your posts, with zero setup. Best for small channels, personal blogs, or quick polls where you don’t need full conversations.
  • Choose @CommentsBot if: You need a full-fledged comment system with threaded replies, user moderation, and high scalability. Perfect for large channels or communities.
  • Choose @ComenBot if: You want comment blocks you can forward to multiple channels or groups, without linking a dedicated group. Good for cross-posting.
  • Choose @Manageil_bot if: You need an all-in-one management tool—comments plus auto-boost, member management, and broadcasting. Overkill for simple reactions, but excellent for active, growing channels.
  • Avoid @ChannelButtonsBot (currently offline) unless it returns.
  • Choose @NoGCommentsBot only if: You have a specific need to restrict comments to group members—otherwise, its low rating and limited use case make it a last resort.

Final Verdict

React Bot fills a clear niche: it’s the minimalist’s choice for adding reactions. While it lacks the depth of @CommentsBot or the management power of @Manageil_bot, its simplicity and speed are unmatched for basic feedback. For most channel admins who just want a "thumbs up" button, @ReactBot is the perfect tool. If you need more, the table above will guide you to a more feature-rich alternative.
