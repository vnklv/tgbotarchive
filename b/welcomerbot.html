Welcome Bots for Telegram: Finding the Right Greeter for Your Group

Welcoming new members to a Telegram group is a crucial first step in building an active community. It sets the tone, provides essential information, and makes newcomers feel valued. Welcome bots automate this process, greeting users with custom messages, rules, or even media. While many bots offer this feature, they vary widely in reliability, complexity, and additional functionalities. This article compares a dedicated greeter, **Welcomer (@welcomerbot)**, against other popular "welcome" category bots to help you choose the right one.

### What Welcome Bots Typically Do

  • At their core, welcome bots listen for new members joining a group and automatically send a predefined message. This message can include:
  • A friendly greeting
  • Group rules or links
  • Buttons for user verification
  • Media like images or GIFs
  • Anti-spam actions (e.g., restricting new users until they click a button)

The best bots do this quickly, reliably, and without flooding the chat with other unwanted features.
