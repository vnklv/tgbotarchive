Overview of Welcomer Bots

At their core, welcomer bots listen for new chat members and send a customizable greeting message. Many also offer features like: - Customizable welcome messages with markdown or HTML - Anti-spam filters (e.g., blocking users with no profile photo) - Auto-deletion of welcome messages after a set time - Support for media attachments (photos, GIFs, stickers) - Integration with group rules or captcha verification

The ideal welcomer bot balances simplicity with enough customization to match a group’s tone.
