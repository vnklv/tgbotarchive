What English Telegram Bots Do

English bots on Telegram typically fall into three categories: - Learning and practice bots: Help users build vocabulary, grammar, or conversational skills through quizzes, games, or repetitive tasks. - Translation bots: Convert text between English and other languages. - Utility bots: Extract text from images or audio, often supporting English alongside other languages.

These bots are especially useful for learners who want to practice on the go, without needing a dedicated app. They leverage Telegram's group and private chat features, making learning social and interactive.
