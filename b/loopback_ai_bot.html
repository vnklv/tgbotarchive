Serverless Telegram Bots Compared: Loopback AI vs. NoPMsBot vs. Fstoriesbot

The "serverless" category in Telegram bots is often misunderstood. In this context, "serverless" doesn’t mean the bots run without servers—rather, these bots provide services that abstract away infrastructure management, allowing users to deploy, run, or interact with virtual environments or simple feedback systems without needing to manage their own servers. They aim to simplify access to computing resources or communication channels. This article compares three bots that fall under this broad umbrella: the ambitious Loopback AI (@loopback_ai_bot), the feedback-oriented NoPMsBot (@NoPMsBot), and the story-sharing Fstoriesbot (@Fstoriesbot).

Overview of the Bots

  • Loopback AI – Described as a way to "launch virtual machines and control them over Telegram-SSH instantly." It promises direct terminal access from your phone, effectively turning Telegram into a remote desktop/SSH client for on-demand VMs.
  • NoPMsBot – Focuses on organizing feedback in forum groups. It creates a separate topic for each user, enabling structured conversations without direct PMs. Highly rated (5.0/5, 671 votes).
  • Fstoriesbot – Allows users to share photos as "stories" via Telegram links. Despite a massive 168,087 votes, it has a very low rating (1.0/5), suggesting widespread dissatisfaction.

How Loopback AI Compares to Others

Loopback AI stands out by offering a true infrastructure-as-a-service experience within Telegram. While NoPMsBot and Fstoriesbot are lightweight, purpose-built tools, Loopback AI attempts to provide a full virtual machine environment. This makes it fundamentally different: it’s not about organizing messages or sharing media—it’s about giving users a remote terminal and compute power. However, its current status ("🔴Flagged as offline") raises immediate concerns about reliability and functionality. In contrast, NoPMsBot appears stable and well-rated, while Fstoriesbot seems to have critical issues given its low rating despite high vote counts.

  • Phone-Centric Control – No need for a separate SSH client or cloud console; everything happens within Telegram. This could appeal to mobile-first users.
